# Unique Items Mới

0.5 bơm 42 unique mới, gắn 3 trục: companion (Spirit Walker), Remnant/Runic Ward, mana-as-resource. Item port từ POE1 (Mageblood, Berek, Loreweave, Geofri's Sanctuary, Facebreaker) re-designed hoàn toàn — tên giống, cơ chế khác, đọc lại từ poedb.

## Companion items

- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Sylvan's_Effigy"} (Stoic Sceptre, Lv62) — "You can have any number of Companions of different types" (phá giới hạn 1 con/loại, không passive/keystone thay được). (50-75)% inc Spirit · Companions deal (50-100)% inc damage to your Marked targets · grants Level 18 Discipline + Level 18 Azmerian Wolf. Enabler của [Tame Beast Companion Pack](/builds/huntress/0-5-spirit-walker-companion-pack).
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Forgotten_Warden"} (Primal Markings, Lv78) — body armour companion-tank base Evasion/ES hybrid, **không player life/res**. 200-300% inc Evasion and ES · +70-100 to Deflection Rating per 50 missing ES (deflection tăng khi ES ăn mòn, max khi ES=0) · "10-15% of Damage from Deflected Hits is taken from Damageable Companion's Life before you" · Companion 30-50% inc max Life · grants Level 18 Spirit Vessel. Roll sample: +90 Deflection/50 missing ES, 285% inc Evasion+ES, 48% companion max life, 11% redirect.
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/The_Raven's_Flock"} (Perching Staff, Lv78, 137 Int) — minion deal (80-120)% inc Damage · (30-50)% inc Spirit Reservation Efficiency of Skills · (10-20)% chance inflict :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Gruelling_Madness"} on Hit · grants Level 19 Spiraling Conspiracy. Reservation efficiency giữ đàn lớn không thiếu Spirit. Từ 0.5.3 bug Spiraling Conspiracy không cập nhật damage khi stat đổi đã fix retroactively.

## Remnant và Runic Ward

- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/The_Hollow_Mask"} (Hewn Mask) — rework gắn Remnant. "Remnants you create affect Allies in your Presence as well as you when collected" + 80-100% inc Reservation Efficiency of Remnant Skills. Đánh đổi -10% to all Elemental Resistances + 15% additional Physical Damage Reduction.
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Eventide_Petals"} (Veridical Chain, Lv78) — amulet Kalguuran duy nhất scale Runic Ward. Implicit +30-40 to maximum Runic Ward (Runic Ward kích ở 1 Life → +40 flat buffer) · grants Level 18 Midnight Zenith · 30-50% inc Critical Hit Chance. Dòng Ice Crystals max life theo Cold Resistance là niche.
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/The_Unleashed"} (Revered Vestments, Lv65, 67 Str/67 Int) — body armour caster Runic Binding. (150-250)% inc Armour and ES · +(10-20) Str and Int · (15-25)% of Damage taken from Hits bypasses ES if ES below half · "Gain 1 Runic Binding on Hit with Spells, no more than once every 0.5 seconds" · "Lose all Runic Bindings when you Shapeshift to gain that much Unbound Potential". Bypass ES = cái giá, không phải feature.
- 0.5.1: craft modifier của *Runemastered Revered Vestments* đổi (10-15% bypass ES → 10-15% increased ES Recharge Rate) — chỉ bản Runemastered, không ảnh hưởng unique The Unleashed gốc.

## Mana và caster

- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Twisted_Empyrean"} (Aberrant Sledge) — mace mana-tank cold. 94% inc Physical Damage · Adds 166-372 Cold Damage · +319 maximum Mana · 10% of Damage is taken from Mana before Life · "Attacks with this Weapon have Added Cold Damage equal to 6-10% of maximum Mana" · Convert 100% of Fire Damage with Mace Skills to Cold.
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Liminal_Coil"} (Twisted Wand, Lv65, 114 Int) — curse-stacking. 71-113% inc Spell Damage · 7-13% inc Cast Speed · "Curses you inflict ignore Curse limit" · Spell Hits Gain 23-31% of Damage as Extra Chaos Damage per Curse on target · Spell Hits Gain 23-31% of Damage as Extra Physical Damage per Curse on target. Roll sample 29% → 3 curse = ~+87% extra Chaos + ~+87% extra Physical (đều ăn Spell Damage scaling).

## Port từ POE1

Không item nào giữ nguyên cơ chế POE1 — đọc lại từ đầu.

- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Mageblood"} (Utility Belt, Lv55) — không còn flask-powerhouse. Mage's Legacy: 4 Legacy grants + "All Mage's Legacies have 25-50% increased effect per duplicate Mage's Legacy you have". Legacy available: Basalt (150% inc Armour), Stibnite (150% inc Evasion Rating), Sulphur (60% inc Damage + Consecrated Ground), Silver (30% inc Skill Speed), Quicksilver (30% Movement Speed), Diamond (75% inc Critical Hit Chance), Gold (45% inc IIR). 4 Legacy/roll ngẫu nhiên. Has 2 Charm Slots + 20% of Flask Recovery applied Instantly.
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Loreweave"} (Ornate Ringmail, Lv43) — "Your Maximum Resistances are (75-80)%". Adds (1-4) to (8-12) Physical Damage to Attacks · +(200-300) Accuracy Rating · +(20-30) maximum Mana · (6-15)% inc Rarity of Items found · +(2-5)% Quality of all Skills · (7-10)% inc Cast Speed · "Random 3 Unique Ring Modifiers".
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Geofri's_Sanctuary"} (Revered Vestments) — "Your maximum Energy Shield is equal to 200-300% of your Strength" · Maximum ES cannot be Converted · 150-200% inc Armour · Regenerate 2 Life per second for every 10 Intelligence. Mỗi 10 Str = 20-30 ES; build 400+ Str → 8k-12k ES từ riêng body.
- Bộ :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Berek's_Grip"} / :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Berek's_Pass"} / :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Berek's_Respite"} (đều Two-Stone Ring, Lv42) — Wind Skills ground surface interaction:
  - Berek's Grip (Cold+Lightning res): "Wind Skills which can be boosted by Elemental Ground Surfaces count as being boosted by Shocked Ground" + Hits against you 35-50% reduced Critical Hit Chance while Chilled.
  - Berek's Pass (Fire+Cold res): Wind Skills count as Chilled Ground + Regenerate 1-2% Life per second while Ignited.
  - Berek's Respite (Fire+Lightning res): Wind Skills count as Ignited Ground + 15-25% inc Critical Damage Bonus while Shocked.
  - BiS cho build Wind Skills muốn lock ground type không cần setup ground thật; ngoài niche đó chỉ là two-stone thường.
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Facebreaker"} (Stocky Mitts, Lv1) — glove unarmed. "1% more Unarmed Damage per 5 Strength" · +0.3m Melee Strike Range while Unarmed · +1 to Armour per Strength · "Has 8 to 12 Physical Damage +3 to +4 per Boss's Face Broken" · 30-50% inc Stun Buildup · "Can Attack as though using a One Handed Mace while both hand slots are empty". Flat damage tăng theo số boss giết (boss-progression scaling).
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Voices"} (Sapphire Jewel, Lv20, corrupted) — "Allocates 4 Sinister Jewel sockets". Corrupted, không Runemaster.

## Items đáng chú ý khác

- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/The_Ordained"} (Grand Spear, Lv79) — spear crit-leech. 243% inc Physical Damage · Adds 1-209 Lightning Damage · +6.47% Critical Hit Chance · 25% inc Melee Strike Range · "Life Leech recovers based on your Lightning damage as well as Physical damage" · "Create a Fragment of Divinity in your Presence every 4 seconds".
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/The_Auspex"} (Exquisite Vest, Lv65) — body evasion. (210-240)% inc Evasion Rating · +(70-120) to maximum Life · grants Level 18 Mist Raven · "Enemies in your Presence gain 1 Gruelling Madness each second" · **"100% increased Attribute Requirements"** (nhân đôi req mọi gear → ép gần thuần Dex).
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Nightfall"} (Glacial Fortress, Lv78) — shield armour cold converter. 150-200% inc Armour · 10-20% inc Block chance · Cold Resistance · 10-20% of Fire damage taken as Cold · 10-20% of Lightning damage taken as Cold. Str cao (~249 sample), grants Level 18 Soaring Midnight + Raise Shield.

## Anti-patterns

- ✗ Mua Mageblood vì nhớ flask POE1 → belt không bật flask; giá trị ở 4 Legacy grants (roll cụm tốt như Sulphur + Silver + Quicksilver).
- ✗ Loreweave để push max-res mà bỏ qua Mana Flask→Guard + Poison→Chill contribution (đổi build direction).
- ✗ Mua Berek ring chỉ vì res không chạy Wind Skills → trục Wind Skills interaction là main draw, res chỉ secondary.

## Version History

### Patch 0.5.3 (2026-06-19)
Fix bug Spiraling Conspiracy của The Raven's Flock không cập nhật damage khi stat đổi. His Grave Command của :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/The_Unborn_Lich"} bỏ Spirit cost; quality gem đổi sang cộng Minion Life thay Reservation Efficiency.

### Patch 0.5.1 (2026-06-05)
- Runemastered Revered Vestments: craft modifier đổi từ "10-15% of Damage taken from Hits bypasses ES if ES below half" → "10-15% increased ES Recharge Rate". Ảnh hưởng bản Runemastered của The Unleashed + Geofri's Sanctuary, không unique gốc.

### Patch 0.5.0 (Return of the Ancients, 2026-05-29)
- +42 unique. The Hollow Mask rework gắn Remnant (sharing + reservation efficiency). Mageblood → Mage's Legacy. Berek 3 ring Wind Skills ground interaction.
