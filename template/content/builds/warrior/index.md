POE2 Warrior — Str melee: armour, block, warcry, đánh chậm-nặng. Melee POE2 thiết kế đánh-chuẩn hơn đánh-nhanh, mỗi swing tốn stamina/mana đáng kể, miss-time là chết. (Khác Marauder POE1 ở triết lý damage.)

## Builds

- [Titan Bear Slam](/builds/warrior/0-5-titan-bear-slam) — Talisman→Bear shapeshift slam, aftershock stacking + Heavy Stun, league-starter armour tank (đông nhất ladder 0.5).
- [Shield Wall Warbringer](/builds/warrior/shield-wall-warbringer) — shield-as-weapon (armour = explosion damage), warcry engine không cooldown, campaign + early endgame.

## Ascendancy

- **Warbringer** — warcry-focus, scale damage qua warcry exertion. Leveling/campaign mạnh.
- **Titan** — defensive/offensive scaling endgame (respec từ Warbringer).
- **Smith** — crafting/gear-oriented (respec).
