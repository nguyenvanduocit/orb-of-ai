# Talisman crafting

:wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Talisman"} = two-handed weapon cho Druid, unlock shapeshift Bear/Werewolf/Wyvern. Physical attack builds (Bear slam, Werewolf strike) scale trực tiếp từ weapon physical damage → weapon pDPS là stat đáng đầu tư nhất. Sau khi Recombinator disabled 0.5.0, craft :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Maji_Talisman"} thu còn 3 hướng.

## Base: Maji Talisman

- **Maji Talisman**: level 79 (100 Str, 67 Int), base physical 61-114, APS 1.25, crit 8% → base pDPS **109.4** trước mod.
- **Fury Talisman** (level 59): base pDPS 87.5. Chênh ~22 pDPS nhân qua %physical mods = khoảng cách không bù. Fury chỉ dùng khi attribute chưa đủ equip Maji.
- Implicit Maji = **+7-10 Maximum Rage** sau 0.5.0 (nerf từ +8-12; implicit cũ reroll bằng Divine Orb).
- **ilvl 75+ bắt buộc** để pool T1 flat physical damage; base <75 không có T1 → ceiling thấp.
- Socket: nhiều socket = nhiều rune slot; ưu tiên socket count cao nhất trong tầm giá.

## 3 method sau 0.5.0

0.5.0 disable Recombinator + xóa Omen of Recombination → method ghép T1 flat phys + T4 %phys (base 550-650 pDPS) không còn.
- **Budget Hybrid** (~1 divine, 450-550 pDPS): magic base có hybrid %physical → Abrasion thêm flat phys → unveil attack speed qua Jawbone.
- **Mua magic base + finish** (~3-4 divine, 600-650 pDPS): mua T1-T2 %phys trade rồi finish.
- **Fracture min-max** (5+ divine, 700+ pDPS): fractured T2 %phys → chaos spam T1 flat phys. Ceiling cao nhất.

## Budget hybrid (~1 divine)

Entry point early mapping.
1. Mua magic Maji có hybrid %physical, **filter "NOT accuracy"** (hybrid accuracy là mod riêng). ~80% increased đủ, không cần T1.
2. :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Greater_Essence_of_Abrasion"}: magic → rare + guarantee prefix flat physical. Two-handed: Greater Abrasion add 23-35 to 39-59 physical. Lock 1 prefix slot.
3. Amamanu unveil attack speed: :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Ancient_Jawbone"} (hoặc tier cao hơn) add Desecrated modifier → unveil tại Amamanu. Amamanu 3 options Talisman (attack speed 18%, spirit reservation reduction, 1 kém) → 1/3 mỗi lần. Fail → scour suffix + Jawbone lại.
4. Finish Battle Essence (cuối, cần prefix trống) → Vaal Infuser push quality. Kết quả ~450-550 pDPS.
- **Source base nhanh hơn**: mua 20-30 white Maji base (~4x/base), spam Perfect Transmute → Perfect Augment. Hybrid %phys chiếm ~15-20% batch → lọc cao nhất; T1 flat phys base scour + resell (không dùng sau Recombinator chết).

## Mua magic base + finish (~3-4 divine)

Mua magic Maji T2+ %physical (90%+). T2 ~3 divine, T1 (130%+) cao hơn. Nhanh nhất mid-game.
1. Greater Essence of Abrasion → rare + guarantee flat phys prefix.
2. Suffix Exalt lock 1 suffix trước unveil (tránh Jawbone overwrite).
3. Jawbone + Amamanu unveil → attack speed 18%.
4. Muốn Gain Physical as Extra prefix → Omen Dextral Erasure + Perfect Gain Physical Essence (phần dưới).
5. Battle Essence cuối → +5 Melee Skill Levels.
6. Vaal Infuser push quality 25%+.
- Kết quả: 600-650 pDPS (T2); T1 %phys đẩy cao hơn.

## Fracture min-max (5+ divine)

Ceiling 700-800 pDPS; mỗi fail fracture mất base → buffer ≥3-4 lần thử.
1. Mua 3-socket Maji white ilvl 75+ (~20x/base). Spam Perfect Nullification + Perfect Augmentation → T2 %physical (Tyrannical — 160%+). T1 quá khó random; T2 đủ (fractured không bị Chaos overwrite).
2. Greater Abrasion → T3 flat phys prefix. Add 1 suffix craft cho đủ mods. Fracturing Orb: fracture đúng %phys = **1/3** (weapon 3 mods). Fail → base die.
3. Sau fracture: Annul off suffix thừa giữ 1 → spam Chaos Orb tới T1 flat physical (61-90 added). Fractured prefix không bị overwrite.
4. Finish Gain Physical as Extra + Battle Essence +5 + Vaal Infuser. Perfect: T2 %phys fractured (160%+), T1 flat phys (61-90), Gain Physical, +5 Melee, 18% attack speed, 25%+ quality → **750-800 pDPS**.

## Ép Gain Physical as Extra

Prefix "Gain X% of Physical Damage as Extra" = mod offensive cao nhất, không roll random thường. Combo :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Omen_of_Dextral_Erasure"} + Perfect Gain Physical Essence:
1. Exalt 1 suffix → còn đúng 1 suffix trống (Dextral Erasure cần suffix để xóa).
2. Omen of Dextral Erasure (~165x) → xóa 1 suffix random, giải phóng slot.
3. Ngay sau: Perfect Essence of Abrasion (Gain Physical variant) → guarantee Gain Physical prefix cả trên rare.
- Expensive (~165x Omen) → chỉ trên base đã T1-T2 %phys + flat phys tốt. Waste vào budget base không thu hồi.

## Battle Essence + Vaal Infuser

- **Battle Essence**: guarantee prefix "+5 to Level of all Melee Skill Gems" trên two-handed 0.5.0 (nerf +7→+5). Luôn **bước cuối chuỗi prefix** (cần prefix trống; full → fail, mất tiền). Check prefix count trước.
- :wiki-link{url="https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Vaal_Infuser"}: add quality, 5/6 không corrupt, 1/6 corrupt (block craft tiếp). Spam tới corrupt, thường 24-27% quality. Mỗi 1% quality = 1% base physical damage. Sau khi finish mọi mod.

## Lỗi hay gặp

- ✗ Mua Fury Talisman vì rẻ → floor pDPS thấp ~20% ngay từ base, không bù bằng roll.
- ✗ Không filter "NOT accuracy" → hybrid accuracy giống %physical tooltip nhưng khác mod, lãng phí Abrasion + Jawbone.
- ✗ Base <ilvl 75 → T1 flat physical không có pool <75.
- ✗ Battle Essence khi prefix full → fail, mất essence + tiền. Luôn cuối chuỗi prefix.
- ✗ Rush Fracture chưa đủ buffer → trung bình 3 lần thử (60x base cost chưa tính roll T2). Chỉ vào khi buffer ≥4-5 lần.
